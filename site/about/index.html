About

About qos

qos — தமிழ் OS. q is how eTamil writes the Tamil letter த.

Why qos

Far more people own an Android phone than a computer. qos turns that phone into a real development machine, so anyone can learn to program and build software, in Tamil or in English, without buying a laptop.

Built on open source

qos is built on Termux, and qos Desktop on Termux:X11, both GPLv3. The tools inside come from the Termux package collections. Thank you to everyone who makes them.

Privacy

qos has no analytics and no telemetry, and needs no account. This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites.

Licence

qos and qos Desktop are free software under the GNU General Public License v3. The source is on GitHub: qos, qos Desktop, this website.
